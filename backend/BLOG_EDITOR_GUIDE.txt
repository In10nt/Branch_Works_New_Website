📝 BLOG EDITOR FIELDS:

1. TITLE *
   - Example: "Offshore vs. In-House Assistants: Smarter Support for Buyer's Agents"
   - Colons (:) will create line breaks on the website

2. URL SLUG *
   - Example: "offshore-vs-inhouse-assistants"
   - This is the URL path: yoursite.com/blog/offshore-vs-inhouse-assistants

3. AUTHOR NAME *
   - Example: "Branchworks Team"
   - Shows at the bottom of the blog

4. CATEGORY *
   - Choose from: Finance, Technology Support, Offshore Hiring
   - Shows as a badge at the top of the blog

5. EXCERPT (Short Description) *
   - Example: "Running a successful buyer's agent business in Australia means managing far more than just inspections and negotiations."
   - Shows in blog listings and as the intro paragraph

6. FEATURED IMAGE *
   - Upload JPG, PNG, or WebP
   - Shows at the top of the blog detail page
   - Image is automatically saved to the server

7. CONTENT *
   - Write your blog content using simple formatting:
   
   FORMATTING GUIDE:
   
   ### Heading
   Creates a section heading (like "What In-House Assistants Do Well")
   
   - List item
   - Another item
   - Third item
   Creates bullet points
   
   Regular paragraph text
   Just write normally for paragraphs
   
   Leave blank lines between sections

8. STATUS *
   - Draft: Not visible on website (for working on blogs)
   - Published: Visible on website (live to public)

📋 EXAMPLE BLOG CONTENT:

### What In-House Assistants Do Well

In-house assistants are best suited for roles that require a physical presence or hands-on involvement in daily operations. If your buyer's agency relies on face-to-face contact or has a team working from a central office, having someone on-site can be incredibly valuable.

You might consider in-house support if:

- You need help setting up or attending property inspections
- Your marketing or client delivery involves printed materials and local logistics
- You want someone who can jump into ad hoc, in-person tasks

However, this model comes with overhead, such as salary, super, equipment, and workspace all adding up. And most in-house assistants are only available during standard business hours, which limits flexibility during evenings or weekends when buyers are often most active.

### Why Offshore Teams Are Growing in Popularity

Offshore virtual assistants offer a different value proposition. They're not physically present, but they excel at the digital, repeatable tasks that eat up your time without requiring face-to-face interaction.
